Jierui (Jerry) Zuo

Hi! Welcome to Jerry’s homepage!

I’m a first-year Ph.D. student in Information Systems at the University of Washington.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, management science, and information systems. I study how AI systems learn and make decisions, and how people and organizations should evaluate, oversee, and delegate decisions to them. My current interests include AI agents, human–AI decision-making, and learning and experimentation on digital platforms. Drawing on online learning, optimization, and experimental design, I develop methods for making decisions under uncertainty and study their implications for the design and use of AI systems.

Before joining UW, I earned my bachelor’s degree in Information Systems from the Department of Management Science and Engineering at Tsinghua University. During my undergraduate studies, I worked closely with Prof. Hanzhang Qin, Prof. Ruihao Zhu, Prof. Bo Li, and Prof. Jinglong Zhao. I also spent a rewarding semester as an exchange student at McGill University, where I studied mathematics and reinforcement learning.

Feel free to reach out to chat about research, exchange ideas, or explore potential collaborations!
