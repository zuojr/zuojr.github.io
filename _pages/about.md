Hi! Welcome to Jerry's homepage!

I'm a first-year Ph.D. student in Information Systems at the [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/).

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, management science, and information systems. I study how AI systems learn and make decisions, and how people and organizations should evaluate, oversee, and delegate decisions to them. My current interests include AI agents, human–AI decision-making, and learning and experimentation on digital platforms. Drawing on online learning, optimization, and experimental design, I develop methods for making decisions under uncertainty and study their implications for the design and use of AI systems.

Before joining UW, I earned my bachelor's degree in Information Systems from the Department of Management Science and Engineering at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/). During my undergraduate studies, I worked closely with [Prof. Hanzhang Qin](https://hanzhangqin.com/), [Prof. Ruihao Zhu](https://rzhu.github.io/), [Prof. Bo Li](https://www.sem.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1219/6985.htm), and [Prof. Jinglong Zhao](https://www.bu.edu/cise/profile/jinglong-zhao/). I also spent a rewarding semester as an exchange student at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/), where I studied mathematics and reinforcement learning.

Feel free to [reach out](mailto:jzuo4@uw.edu) to chat about research, exchange ideas, or explore potential collaborations!
